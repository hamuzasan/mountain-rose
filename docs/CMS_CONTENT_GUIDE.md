## Image Guidelines

Product image guidelines:

- Use clean product photos.
- Prefer warm neutral backgrounds.
- Show leather texture clearly.
- Use consistent lighting.
- Avoid messy marketplace-style photos.
- Avoid overexposed white backgrounds.
- Avoid low-resolution images.
- Use square or 4:5 vertical ratio for product cards.
- Use wider editorial photos for hero sections.

Alt text should describe the actual image, product, color, and angle when possible. Example: `Rosewood Tote warna cognac tampak depan di latar warm ivory.`

## Copywriting Guidelines

Mountain Rose copy should sound:

- Elegant
- Calm
- Confident
- Refined
- Trustworthy

Avoid:

- Terlalu banyak emoji
- Kata-kata diskon berlebihan
- Gaya marketplace murah
- Klaim berlebihan
- Bahasa terlalu ramai

Good example:

> Tas kulit sapi asli dengan karakter alami, dibuat untuk menemani perjalanan panjang.

Bad example:

> Tas kulit murah banget, kualitas premium super keren, buruan order!!!

## WhatsApp Order Message Guidelines

Product WhatsApp messages should be concise, polite, and specific.

Example for a product:

> Halo Mountain Rose, saya tertarik dengan produk Rosewood Tote. Apakah masih tersedia?

Example for custom product:

> Halo Mountain Rose, saya ingin konsultasi tas kulit custom. Apakah bisa dibantu untuk pilihan model dan warna?

Avoid urgency, excessive punctuation, slang, and discount language.

## SEO Content Tips

- Use a clear product name and slug.
- Keep short descriptions natural and readable.
- Mention `kulit sapi asli` only when relevant and truthful.
- Do not repeat keywords unnaturally.
- Use image alt text for accessibility and search context.
- Keep article excerpts practical and calm.
- Write content for people first, search engines second.
