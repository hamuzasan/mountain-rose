# Mountain Rose Brand Guide

## Positioning

Mountain Rose is a premium genuine cow leather bag brand for customers who value quiet elegance, durable materials, refined craftsmanship, and timeless daily style. The brand combines the calm strength of mountain landscapes with the restrained beauty of a mature rose-inspired identity.

## Brand Voice

Mountain Rose speaks with a voice that is:

- Elegant
- Calm
- Confident
- Refined
- Premium
- Trustworthy

Use measured language. Let quality, material, and craft carry the message.

## Indonesian Copywriting Tone

Indonesian copy should feel polished, warm, and mature. Use clear sentences, refined vocabulary, and a confident boutique tone.

Prefer:

- "Tas kulit sapi asli dengan karakter yang semakin indah seiring waktu."
- "Dirancang untuk menemani hari-hari penting dengan kesan elegan yang tenang."
- "Detail jahitan rapi, pilihan material matang, dan bentuk yang tidak lekang oleh tren."

Avoid:

- Slang
- Excessive hype
- Too many emojis
- Cheap discount language
- Marketplace-style wording
- Pushy urgency or exaggerated claims

## Good Copy Examples

- "Keanggunan yang tenang, dibuat dari kulit sapi asli pilihan."
- "Setiap detail dirancang untuk menghadirkan tas yang kuat, indah, dan layak dikenakan bertahun-tahun."
- "Warna kulit yang hangat, siluet yang matang, dan sentuhan rose yang halus."
- "Untuk perempuan yang memilih kualitas tanpa perlu tampil berlebihan."

## Bad Copy Examples

- "PROMO GILA!!! Tas kulit murah banget, buruan checkout!!!"
- "Super cute pink flower bag yang wajib kamu punya!!!"
- "Best seller viral, jangan sampai ketinggalan ya sis."
- "Diskon bombastis khusus hari ini, stok tinggal dikit banget!"

## Talking About Genuine Cow Leather

Use honest, specific, and confident language:

- Say "kulit sapi asli" or "genuine cow leather" when referring to the material.
- Highlight natural grain, durability, texture, and character.
- Explain that leather can develop a richer patina with proper care.
- Avoid overpromising, fake luxury claims, or vague material language.

Good examples:

- "Dibuat dari kulit sapi asli dengan tekstur alami yang memberi karakter pada setiap tas."
- "Material kulit dipilih untuk ketahanan, struktur, dan kesan hangat yang matang."

## Talking About Elegance, Durability, and Craftsmanship

Elegance should be quiet, not flashy. Durability should feel practical and trustworthy. Craftsmanship should be described through details.

Prefer language about:

- Refined silhouettes
- Balanced proportions
- Careful stitching
- Strong handles and structure
- Long-lasting material
- Warm leather tones
- Timeless styling

Avoid inflated claims such as "terbaik nomor satu", "paling mewah sedunia", or language that feels like a discount marketplace listing.
